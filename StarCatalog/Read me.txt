NIGHTLENS OFFLINE SKY MAP DATA

5044 stars through apparent magnitude 6, 150 constellation line chains and
89 label positions (including split Serpens) adapted into stars.json from:
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial/tree/master/data

Upstream readme.md is retained with detailed original data provenance.
Stars: XHIP, An Extended Hipparcos Compilation (Anderson & Francis, 2012),
VizieR V/137D. Names/designations: VizieR IV/27A and upstream name records.
Constellation positions/lines: IAU-based d3-celestial data, modified by Olaf
Frohn. Only English/common labels and required numerical fields are retained.

Modifications for NightLens: converted GeoJSON points into compact JSON;
normalized right ascension to 0–360 degrees; retained apparent magnitude,
selected common/Bayer names, line chains and English constellation labels.
Positions remain J2000. No invented stars or decorative random points are used.

The application draws a target-centered gnomonic projection, north up / east
left. Field width defaults to 60 degrees; the mouse wheel changes it from 15
to 90 degrees. Double-click opens the larger map. Moon/planet centers use
Astronomy Engine topocentric J2000 coordinates at the displayed best time.
The map is for finding the region, not precision mount alignment. It omits
proper motion updates, terrain/horizon obstructions and atmospheric refraction.
It works offline. Background stars do not represent the current naked-eye
visibility under your local Moon/cloud/light-pollution conditions.

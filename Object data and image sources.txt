NIGHTLENS 7 — OBJECT INFORMATION AND REFERENCE IMAGES

CATALOG DATA
OpenNGC by Mattia Verga and contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC
https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC/blob/master/NGC_guide.txt
The bundled catalog credits/license remain in Catalogs. This release retains
V/B/J/H/K magnitudes, B-band mean surface brightness, morphology and catalog
notes through cross-catalog merging. Missing values are not synthesized.
For custom catalogs, Magnitude without a named band stays band unspecified.
Angular size is the catalog major/minor extent, not a measured live diameter.
Descriptions combine object classification and computed constellation with
brief general explanations; they are not invented object-specific histories.

EPHEMERIDES
Astronomy Engine by Don Cross, MIT license (source/license in Source).
https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy
J2000 catalog positions are precessed for altitude calculations; moving bodies
use topocentric apparent coordinates of date. Surface refraction uses the
library's normal model. Terrain/trees and an exact local atmospheric profile
are not modeled. Refined best time is the highest 1-minute altitude sample near
the coarse best sample inside the uninterrupted recommended window.

DIGITIZED SKY SURVEY
Online deep-sky cutouts: STScI / Digitized Sky Survey, DSS2 red.
https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/
https://gsss.stsci.edu/Software/WebServices.htm
https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/faq.html
https://gsss.stsci.edu/Acknowledgements/DSSCopyrights.htm
DSS images were produced at the Space Telescope Science Institute under
U.S. Government grant NAG W-2166. Survey plates originate from the Palomar
Observatory Sky Survey and UK Schmidt Telescope surveys. Underlying plate
copyright holders vary; see the STScI acknowledgment and copyright pages.
This personal astronomy planner requests images on demand and caches them
locally. The package does not bulk-redistribute survey images. Survey cutouts
are historical reference fields with no guaranteed match to a user's camera
orientation, filters, exposure or displayed apparent sky brightness.

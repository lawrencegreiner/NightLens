NightLens changes:
- Split all 13,970 source NGC/IC records into separate NGC.csv and IC.csv.
- Converted CSV quoting / UTF-8 serialization; retained source columns/values.
- Derived the Messier selection from cross-identifiers and the supplement.
- Replaced the disputed M102 duplicate-M101 mapping in that derived Messier
  selection with NGC5866, following NASA Hubble's Messier presentation:
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-102/
  The derived row documents this choice. Original NGC/IC values are kept.
- Copied the complete 64-row supplement into its own selectable file.
